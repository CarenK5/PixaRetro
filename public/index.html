How pricing works

You set the budget. Pros quote within it.

There's no fixed price list — every shoot is different. Set what you're willing to spend, and PixaRetro surfaces pros whose rate already fits it.

STEP 01

Set your budget

Tell us your content type and what you're comfortable spending — no need to guess a "going rate."

STEP 02

See who fits it

We only surface pros whose quoted rate is at or under your number, portfolio included.

STEP 03

One clear price

Whatever rate you agree on with your pro is what you pay. No line items, no surprise charges added at checkout.

Pros set their own rate on their portfolio. You'll always see the exact quote before you book — nothing is charged until you approve.
